## Summary
The User Interface Designer and the Industrial Designer presented two prototypes, one with an LCD screen and one without. They decided to continue working on the design and to take out the LCD screen and voice recognition. The group was pleased with the teamwork and leadership, but one participant complained that not enough information was given about each meeting's agenda.

## Key Points
- The Industrial Designer presented two remote control prototypes with different button configurations and features.
- The group discussed the costs of production, noting that the current prototype is above the budget.
- The team evaluated the remote based on fancy look, innovation, and ease of use.
- The Marketing Expert suggested that the product should be more customized and follow fruit or vegetable shapes.
- The group discussed using a spongy material for the remote to make it more comfortable to hold.
- The team explored a modular design where users can add components like a speech recogniser or parental control.
- The group debated whether to include the LCD screen because it might increase production costs without adding significant value.
- The team discussed the possibility of offering different skins for the remote to allow for user customisable options.

## Decisions
- The remote will feature rechargeable double-A batteries and a charging stand.
- The remote can be customized for adults, children, or the elderly.
- The remote has a misplacement reminder that sends a message to the television screen to remind users to put the remote back in the charger when not in use.
- The remote features a modular design that allows users to add as many or as few components as they wish to the basic model.
- The group was unsatisfied with the design and decided to continue working on it, focusing on a fancier design.
- The remote will not feature an LCD screen or voice recognition.
- The group decided that the teamwork and leadership of the project were good, but that not enough information was given about each meeting's agenda.

## Summary
The project manager opened the meeting and introduced the project, to design a remote control. The remote should be trendy, original and user friendly. The group discussed initial ideas for the remote, including that it should be a simple design with grouped buttons. The project manager went over the roles of the participants and closed the meeting.

## Key Points
- The project aim is to design a new remote control.
- The team practiced using the smart-boards and white-board tools.
- The group discussed the importance of grouped buttons for usability.
- The team talked about the importance of the design being original and trendy.

## Decisions
- The group will design a trendy, original and user friendly television remote control.
- The remote will be sold for 25 Euros.
- It will cost no more than 12.5 Euros to make.
- The profit aim is 15 million Euros.
- The remote will be sold internationally.

## Summary
The project manager opened the meeting and stated the agenda to the team members. The marketing expert discussed the findings of a survey which indicated that current remotes are ugly, difficult to use, have a number of unused buttons, frustrate users when misplaced, and contribute to RSI. After the project manager's closing, the project manager recapped some decisions and the team discussed how to handle the issue of locating a remote when misplaced.

## Key Points
- The Marketing Expert presented survey results showing that current remote controls are often ugly and difficult to use.
- Users expressed frustration when remotes are lost and requested features to help locate them.
- The team discussed the potential for speech recognition and the technical challenges of implementing it.
- The User Interface Designer proposed a simple design using a titanium body and a screen with a back light.
- The Industrial Designer explained the basic electronic components and infrared technology needed for the device.
- The group discussed the feasibility of adding a speaker or clap-activated features for finding the remote.
- The team considered targeting young people as early adopters of new technology.
- The group discussed the trade-offs between a universal remote and a remote for only TV.

## Decisions
- The team will not work with teletext.
- The team will concentrate on making a remote only for televisions and then make their product more generalized later.
- An automatic on/off feature will not be used.

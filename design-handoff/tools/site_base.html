The whole meeting, handled locally.

Recording, transcription, clean-up and summaries in one native app. Every part runs on your Mac.

Live transcription

Words appear in silence-aligned chunks while you record. Parakeet and BAPS Whisper for English, Whisper for 99 languages and translate-to-English.

BAPS WhisperParakeetWhisper

Summaries, open models

Ollama by default, or any self-hosted OpenAI-compatible endpoint. Long transcripts are chunked and merged.

  • Ollama default
  • llama.cpp self-hosted
  • vLLM self-hosted
  • LM Studio self-hosted
  • LocalAI self-hosted

Mic and system audio

Captures your microphone and everything the Mac plays, mixed with per-channel gain and soft limiting. Pause and resume.

Microphone
System audio

Speaker labels

After the meeting, the other voices are separated and given stable names. Your microphone is always you.

Transcript clean-up

One click turns raw timestamped output into readable paragraphs with topic headings. Filler removed, timecodes kept, raw one toggle away.

A searchable library

Every meeting stored locally with notes, full-text search and Markdown export.

Import and re-transcribe

Drop in any audio file, or run an existing recording through a different model for a better pass.

Nothing leaves your Mac.

Wffl is local-first, not local-optional. Audio, transcripts and summaries stay in your own file system, and the app works with the network off.

From download to first recording.

Models are fetched once from inside the app, so you choose what lands on your disk.

  1. 01

    Install

    Open the DMG and drag Wffl into Applications. Signed and notarized, so it opens normally.

    drag to Applications
  2. 02

    Download a speech model

    Settings, then Transcription. Start with base.en, or large-v3-turbo for best quality. A few hundred MB to a few GB, one time.

    one time, from inside the app
  3. 03

    Add a local model

    Install Ollama and pull a model for summaries and clean-up. Optional: recording and transcription work without it.

    optional

App icon: the mark, held still.

The old icon was a cream squircle with a dark stroke W. The new one keeps the squircle and the idea of a single centred mark, and swaps the stroke for the eight-bar waveform W. Recommendation: A, Ink.

A · Ink (recommended)

Near-black squircle, off-white bars. Reads at 16px, stands apart from the cream legacy icon, matches the black pill buttons.

B · Paper

Cool off-white squircle, ink bars. Closest to the old icon's cream (the old-to-new story), but low contrast against light Docks and Finder.

C · Ink + ember lamp (not recommended)

An ember dot breaks "ember means live": an app icon is never live. Shown only so it is a decision, not an omission.

Build notes (from QA I-1 to I-6)

Eight capsule bars, heights 1, .67, .33, .67, .87, .67, .33, 1 of the tallest (same as the in-app mark and favicon). Body 824px on the 1024 grid, continuous corners. No ember. No baked shadow or hairline in the shipped art (the shadow in these previews is only for the mockup). Deliver as an Icon Composer .icon with three appearances, plus an .icns fallback.

  • Light appearance (default): A, ink tile #0B0B0B to #0A0A0A, bars #F2F2F0. Alternative if the owner prefers paper: B.
  • Dark appearance: tile #0A0A0A, bars #F2F2F0 (same as A).
  • Tinted: bars only as a single-colour foreground layer on the system tint.
  • Small sizes (16 and 32): keep bar width at least 2px; if bars blur, use a simplified variant with 6 bars. Check at 16, 32, 128.

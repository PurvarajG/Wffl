01 / THE WORDS

Hear it.
See it take shape.

Words appear in silence-aligned chunks while you record. Capture your microphone and Mac audio; pause and resume any time.

Parakeet and BAPS Whisper for English. Whisper for other languages and translation.

02 / THE VOICES

A voice becomes
someone you know.

Other voices start as Speaker 1, Speaker 2. Rename a speaker once; Wffl can recognize that voice in later meetings. Your microphone is always Me.

Try renaming the speaker in the sample.

03 / THE CLARITY

Less noise.
More meaning.

Clean up a transcript into readable paragraphs with topic headings. Keep timecodes and return to the raw version whenever you need it.

With your configured model, on supported engines.

04 / THE TAKEAWAY

The conversation,
distilled.

Turn a long transcript into minutes, decisions and action items. Ollama is the local default. An optional remote endpoint receives transcript text.

05 / THE RETURN

Leave the meeting.
Keep what matters.

Your recordings, transcripts and notes stay in a local library. Search the words, export Markdown, or bring in another audio file.

What stays on your Mac ↓

Your audio stays on your Mac.

Recording and transcription stay on your Mac. With a local summary model, transcripts and summaries stay here too. A remote summary or clean-up endpoint receives transcript text. Once downloaded and selected, local models let Wffl process offline.

From download to first recording.

Models are fetched once from inside the app, so you choose what lands on your disk.

  1. 01

    Install

    Open the DMG and drag Wffl into Applications. Signed and notarized, so it opens normally.

    drag to Applications
  2. 02

    Download a speech model

    In Settings → Transcription, download BAPS Whisper (about 1.6 GB). It is recommended for English with Gujarati and Sanskrit vocabulary in Latin letters. The first load may take a few minutes while it compiles for the Neural Engine. Choose Whisper for other languages or translation.

    one time, from inside the app
  3. 03

    Add a local model

    Install Ollama and download a model suited to your Mac for local summaries and AI transcript clean-up on supported engines. Optional: BAPS Whisper transcription works without Ollama.

    optional

Full details in the setup guide.

Sample meeting, invented for this page.

Press record.

Captures your microphone and everything the Mac plays, mixed with per-channel gain and soft limiting. Pause and resume any time.

via ScreenCaptureKit

Watch it become words.

Words appear in silence-aligned chunks while you record. Parakeet and BAPS Whisper for English, Whisper for 99 languages and translate-to-English.

on the Neural Engine and Metal

Tell the voices apart.

Other voices are separated as Speaker 1, Speaker 2. Rename one once and Wffl recognizes that voice in later meetings. Your microphone is always Me.

with FluidAudio

Clean it up in one click.

Raw timestamped output becomes readable paragraphs with topic headings. Filler removed, timecodes kept, raw one toggle away.

with a local model

Minutes, written locally.

Ollama by default, or any self-hosted OpenAI-compatible endpoint: llama.cpp, vLLM, LM Studio, LocalAI. Long transcripts are chunked and merged.

with Ollama

Keep it. Find it.

Every meeting stored locally with notes, full-text search and Markdown export. Drop in any audio file, or re-transcribe with a different model.

in local SQLite

Your audio stays on your Mac.

Wffl is local-first, not local-optional. Audio, transcripts and summaries stay in your own file system, and the app works with the network off.

From download to first recording.

Models are fetched once from inside the app, so you choose what lands on your disk.

  1. 01

    Install

    Open the DMG and drag Wffl into Applications. Signed and notarized, so it opens normally.

    drag to Applications
  2. 02

    Download a speech model

    Settings, then Transcription. Start with base.en, or large-v3-turbo for best quality. A few hundred MB to a few GB, one time.

    one time, from inside the app
  3. 03

    Add a local model

    Install Ollama and pull a model for summaries and clean-up. Optional: recording and transcription work without it.

    optional
